Privacy Policy — CCAT Trainer

Last updated: 21 September 2026

CCAT Trainer does not collect any data. There is no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party service. The app has no server of its own and sends nothing anywhere.

What is stored, and where

Your practice history — sessions, answers, scores and which questions you have already seen — is written to your device's local browser storage (IndexedDB) and never leaves it. Your language preference is stored the same way. Nobody, including the developer, can read it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. On the web, clearing the site data for this address has the same effect. Because no copy exists anywhere else, deletion is immediate and final.

Permissions

The Android app declares a single permission, INTERNET, which the embedded WebView requires in order to load the app's own local files. The app makes no network requests.

Children

The app is aimed at adults preparing for a hiring assessment. It is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address listed on the app's store page.
